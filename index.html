06 BEFORE YOU ASK

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Small services work best when expectations are set before the first task.

What kind of tasks can I send?+

Small digital business tasks such as formatting, copy cleanup, organizing supplied information, focused research summaries, lead-list cleanup, tailored AI prompts, simple process documentation, or feedback on one web page. If your request is different, ask; I’ll confirm whether I can do it well.

Does every task cost $5?+

No. $5 is for one genuinely small and well-defined task. Bundles and mini projects start at higher prices. You’ll get the agreed scope, price, and delivery timing before any work begins.

Do I need to provide files or account access?+

Share only the information needed for the task. Never send passwords or sensitive customer data. Many jobs can be done from text, screenshots, public links, or sanitized sample data.

Is payment available directly on this website?+

No checkout is connected yet. Send a request first. We’ll confirm scope, availability, the supported payment method, and the delivery window before any work begins.

What if the task is too complex or outside your skills?+

I’ll tell you before starting rather than promise something I can’t deliver. Complex coding, regulated professional advice, tasks needing private account access, and unsupported work may be declined.

Do you guarantee sales or business results?+

No. I guarantee only the deliverable and scope agreed before the task starts, not revenue, leads, rankings, or other business outcomes.
